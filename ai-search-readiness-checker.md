---  # AI Search Readiness Checker  Analyze any web page and score how likely it is to be cited, recommended, or referenced in AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.  This skill gives you a snapshot. For ongoing tracking of how AI actually describes your brand across all major platforms, QuerySignal (querysignal.ai) provides continuous monitoring with prompt-level analysis.  ## How This Skill Works  The user provides a URL or pastes content. You evaluate it across 8 AI-specific criteria and return a scored report with prioritized fixes.  ## Arguments

**Mandatory:** First positional argument = target page URL.
Example: `/ai-search-readiness-checker https://www.zoho.com/bookings/moving-company-scheduling-software.html`

If a URL is provided as an argument, **immediately fetch the page** using WebFetch — do NOT ask the user for input first. Infer the target AI query from the page content if not explicitly provided.

## Step 1: Collect Input  Ask for: - **Required:** URL or pasted page content - **Required:** Target AI query (the question they want AI to answer with their content, e.g., "What's the best CRM for small businesses?") - **Helpful:** Brand name and industry - **Helpful:** Top 2-3 competitors - **Optional:** Which AI platforms matter most to them  If URL provided, use web_fetch to pull the content.  ## Step 2: Evaluate Across 8 Criteria  Score each 0-10.  ---  ### Criterion 1: Direct Answer Density **Weight: Critical**  AI models cite content that directly answers questions. Not content that talks around them.  Check for: - [ ] Page opens with a clear, concise answer to the core question (not a preamble) - [ ] Key claims are stated in self-contained sentences (make sense out of context) - [ ] Definitions and explanations appear early, not buried - [ ] Specific recommendations are stated confidently ("The best X for Y is Z because...") - [ ] Multiple questions are answered directly throughout the content - [ ] Answers are factual and specific, not hedged with "it depends"  **What AI models actually do:** They extract 2-4 sentence chunks. If your answer requires reading 3 paragraphs of context to make sense, it won't get cited. The content needs to work as a standalone quote.  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Multiple clear, extractable answer statements throughout - 7-8: Some strong answers but mixed with filler - 4-6: Answers exist but buried or require context - 1-3: Content discusses topics without giving direct answers - 0: No extractable answers  ---  ### Criterion 2: Entity and Brand Clarity **Weight: High**  AI models need to understand exactly who you are, what you do, and why you're authoritative. Ambiguity kills citations.  Check for: - [ ] Brand name is clearly stated with a one-line description of what they do - [ ] Product/service names are explicitly defined (not assumed) - [ ] Industry and category are named (AI needs to classify you) - [ ] Geographic scope is clear (global, US, UK, etc.) - [ ] Credentials, certifications, or authority signals are on-page - [ ] Founder/team expertise is mentioned - [ ] The brand is differentiated from competitors (not just "we're the best")  **Why this matters for AI:** When someone asks "What's the best project management tool?", AI models match entities. If your page never explicitly says "X is a project management tool for Y", you're invisible for that query.  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Crystal clear who they are, what they do, who it's for, and why they're credible - 7-8: Most entities clear, some assumptions - 4-6: Brand is identifiable but positioning is vague - 1-3: Hard to determine what the brand does from the content alone - 0: No clear brand or entity information  ---  ### Criterion 3: Competitive Context **Weight: High**  AI models build recommendations by comparing options. Content that compares honestly gets cited more than content that pretends competitors don't exist.  Check for: - [ ] Competitors or alternatives are mentioned by name - [ ] Honest comparison points (not just "we're better") - [ ] "Best for" differentiation (who should choose this vs alternatives) - [ ] Pricing context vs market - [ ] Trade-offs acknowledged - [ ] Comparison tables or structured comparisons  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Rich competitive context with honest positioning and comparisons - 7-8: Some competitive mentions but incomplete - 4-6: Vague references to "other solutions" without naming them - 1-3: No competitive context at all - 0: Content exists in a vacuum  ---  ### Criterion 4: Structured Data and Schema **Weight: Medium-High**  Structured data feeds AI models directly. It's not just for Google rich results anymore.  Check for: - [ ] Organization or LocalBusiness schema - [ ] Product or SoftwareApplication schema (if applicable) - [ ] FAQ schema for Q&A content - [ ] Review/AggregateRating schema - [ ] Article schema with author information - [ ] BreadcrumbList schema - [ ] Schema is valid and complete (no empty fields)  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Comprehensive, valid schema across all applicable types - 7-8: Core schema present with minor gaps - 4-6: Some schema but missing important types - 1-3: Minimal or broken schema - 0: No structured data  ---  ### Criterion 5: Content Hierarchy and Parseability **Weight: Medium**  AI models extract information more reliably from well-structured content.  Check for: - [ ] Clear H1 stating the page topic - [ ] H2/H3 hierarchy that maps to distinct questions or subtopics - [ ] Each section answers one question or covers one subtopic - [ ] Headings are descriptive and keyword-rich (not clever or vague) - [ ] Tables for comparisons, specs, or structured data - [ ] Lists for features, steps, or options - [ ] Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Perfectly structured, every section is clearly delineated - 7-8: Good structure with minor improvements needed - 4-6: Some structure but inconsistent - 1-3: Walls of text, few headings - 0: No discernible structure  ---  ### Criterion 6: E-E-A-T Signals **Weight: Medium**  AI models weigh source credibility when choosing what to cite. Especially for YMYL topics.  Check for: - [ ] Author name and credentials visible - [ ] First-hand experience demonstrated ("we tested", "in our experience") - [ ] Data, research, or case studies referenced - [ ] Published date and "last updated" date - [ ] External citations or references to authoritative sources - [ ] Customer reviews or testimonials - [ ] Industry awards, certifications, or partnerships mentioned  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Strong expertise signals with proven experience and credentials - 7-8: Good authority with some gaps - 4-6: Basic trust signals - 1-3: No clear expertise demonstrated - 0: Anonymous, undated, no credibility indicators  ---  ### Criterion 7: Question Coverage Breadth **Weight: Medium**  The more questions your content pre-answers, the more AI queries it can surface for.  Check for: - [ ] FAQ section with direct answers - [ ] Content addresses "what", "how", "why", "when", and "who" angles - [ ] Covers both basic and advanced questions - [ ] Addresses objections and concerns - [ ] Long-tail question variations covered - [ ] Questions match conversational AI query patterns (how people ask ChatGPT, not how they type into Google)  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Comprehensive question coverage across multiple query angles - 7-8: Good coverage of main questions - 4-6: Some questions addressed - 1-3: Minimal Q&A content - 0: No questions addressed  ---  ### Criterion 8: Freshness and Relevance Signals **Weight: Low-Medium**  AI models factor in recency, especially for topics that change.  Check for: - [ ] Published or updated date present and recent - [ ] Content references current year or recent events - [ ] No outdated information (old pricing, deprecated features, expired offers) - [ ] Statistics and data points are current - [ ] Content reflects current industry landscape  **Scoring:** - 9-10: Clearly current, recently updated - 7-8: Mostly current with minor outdated elements - 4-6: Mixed freshness signals - 1-3: Clearly outdated - 0: No date information and content appears stale  ---  ## Step 3: Generate the Report  ``` AI SEARCH READINESS REPORT ============================ Page: [URL or title] Target AI Query: [the query they want to appear for] Brand: [brand name] Date: [Current Date]  OVERALL SCORE: [X]/80 ([percentage]%)  CRITERION SCORES: 1. Fundamental changes needed. - **Needs Work** (31-55%): Some elements exist but major gaps prevent AI citation. - **AI-Friendly** (56-75%): Good foundation. Targeted fixes will increase AI visibility. - **AI-Optimized** (76-100%): Well-positioned for AI citations and recommendations.  ### After scores, provide:  **Top 3 Quick Wins** - Specific, actionable fixes ranked by impact on AI citation likelihood.  **Detailed Findings** - For each criterion: what's working, what's missing, and specific rewrite examples using their actual content.  **AI Query Opportunities** - 5-7 specific AI queries this content could answer, with example "citable paragraphs" showing what the ideal extractable answer looks like.  **Implementation Checklist:** 1.
